# Citations

References and influences acknowledged by FEDMON: NO MERCY.

This is a creative project; the citations below acknowledge the works that shaped its design, aesthetic, and ethos. No code or assets are directly copied from these sources unless explicitly stated in [COPYING.md](COPYING.md).

---

## Design influences

The monster-taming action RPG genre, broadly. FEDMON: NO MERCY is a deliberate **adult reimagining** of creature-collector conventions — it pays homage to the genre while rejecting its softness.

Specific inspirations for mechanics (not assets):
- **Creature collectors** — the "raise, evolve, battle" loop, recontextualized with consequences (debt, arrest, no free healing).
- **Survival/economy sims** — the daily-burn and property-ownership mechanics.
- **Crime/life-sim games** — the heat system, loan sharks, and jurisdiction framing.
