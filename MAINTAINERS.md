## Maintainer responsibilities

- **Triage issues and PRs** within a reasonable window; label with `needs-triage` then the appropriate category.
- **Enforce the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).** Brutal game, kind community.
- **Guard the aesthetic.** Reject restyled clones of prior layouts. Reject pastel/soft rewrites. The brutalist red/black/monospace tone is the product.
- **Guard the constraints.** No frameworks, no build steps, no external core assets, no secrets, no racially coded naming.
- **Review for accessibility.** `prefers-reduced-motion`, coarse-pointer cursor disabling, responsive breakpoints.
- **Keep easter eggs alive** (F-key glitch, console logs) unless explicitly removed by the lead.
- **Verify before merge.** Open the page in a browser; confirm no console errors; confirm responsive behavior.

## How to become a maintainer

Consistent, high-quality merged contributions over time + alignment with the project's constraints and tone. Open a discussion if you're interested.
