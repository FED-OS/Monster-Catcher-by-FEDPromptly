## Non-goals (explicitly not doing)

- ❌ Free healing. Ever. It's a core identity pillar.
- ❌ Softening the aesthetic for mass appeal.
- ❌ Racially coded lore, names, or locations.
- ❌ Microtransactions / loot mechanics that mirror the in-fiction loan sharks.
