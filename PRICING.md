# Pricing

How FEDMON: NO MERCY is (and isn't) monetized.

---

## The game

**FEDMON: NO MERCY** will be a **paid, one-time-purchase** indie game. No free-to-play, no energy systems, no pay-to-win, no loot boxes.

| Edition | Price (target) | Includes |
|---------|----------------|----------|
| Standard | $14.99 | Full game, all regions, all creatures |
| Supporter | $24.99 | Standard + supporter badge + digital art book + soundtrack |

Prices are targets for the full release and may shift during development. Pre-alpha playtests are free.

### What we will NOT do

- ❌ **Microtransactions** — the in-game loan sharks are satire; we won't run real ones.
- ❌ **Loot boxes / gacha** — creatures are caught, bred, and traded in-world, not gambled for with real money.
- ❌ **Energy/stamina gates bought with cash** — the in-game stamina economy is a gameplay constraint, never a monetization lever.
- ❌ **Paywalled content** — no "buy the second half." One purchase, full game.
- ❌ **Ads** — never.

The game is brutal about money *in-fiction*. We won't be brutal about your real wallet.

## In-fiction economy (for clarity)

The game's brutal economy — daily burn costs, loan shark interest, banking wire fees (15%), foreclosure, fines doubling per offense — is **gameplay**, not a metaphor for real-world monetization. You pay in-game currency earned in-game. The cruelty is the point of the *game*, not the *business*.

See [README.md](README.md) §Economy and the in-page `#econ` section for the in-fiction breakdown.
