# Heat and Arrest

FEDMON: NO MERCY models legal consequences. The police are a system, not a cutscene.

## The heat meter

Fight unprovoked too often and your **heat** rises. The promo page's left rail HUD shows a live `HEAT` readout (spiking 0–9) as a nod to this.

- Heat accumulates from unprovoked aggression, disturbances, and certain black-market activity.
- High heat draws police attention.

## Arrest

When heat trips the threshold, you get **arrested**. Consequences:

- **Cash halved** — they take what's on you.
- **Car towed** — you lose fast-travel until you recover it.
- **Court fines double every offense** — repeat arrests get exponentially more expensive.

This is why **banking your money in the PC** matters (see [Economy](Economy.md)). Money in the bank isn't on you when you're busted. Money in your pocket is.

## Surviving a bust

- Bank ahead of time.
- Keep a cash buffer you can afford to lose.
- Avoid unprovoked fights when heat is already high.
- Property ownership can play into legal standing (proof of address) — but missed payments are their own risk.

## Design intent

Most creature-collectors have no legal system. FEDMON's arrest mechanic makes aggression a *calculated* choice rather than a default. You can be a menace — but the world pushes back, hard, and progressively.

It also reinforces the economy: arrest is primarily a **financial** consequence. The cruelty is fiscal.
