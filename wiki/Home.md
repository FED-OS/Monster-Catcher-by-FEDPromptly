## What FEDMON is

A gritty, adult-oriented monster-taming action RPG (Godot 4). You raise creatures and fight alongside them — but the world doesn't coddle you. Healing costs money. Fists are a weapon. Creatures get repossessed. The police track your heat. The loan shark wants his money.

**No gyms. No badges. No free healing.**
