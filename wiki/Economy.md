# Economy

The economy is the beating, broken heart of FEDMON: NO MERCY. Money is survival. Healing is not free.

## No free healing

The single most important rule. Healing costs money — always. There is no infinite free clinic. Every fight you take is a financial decision: can you afford to patch up afterward?

This transforms combat from a pure tactics puzzle into a risk/reward calculation. You don't just ask "can I win?" — you ask "can I win *and still afford to be alive tomorrow*?"

## Daily burn

Survival has a daily cost (food, shelter, upkeep). The promo page's `#econ` section shows a receipt-style "DAILY BURN" panel. If you don't earn enough, you bleed cash just by existing.

## Property ownership

- Buy property for **proof of address** (a legal requirement in-world).
- New properties cost **double your income** — forces backtracking and planning.
- Miss payments? **Foreclosure, repossession, seizure.** Your stuff can be taken.
- Property is both a status tool and a liability.

## Banking

- Bank your money in the PC to protect it (e.g. from arrest confiscation).
- Wire transfers carry a **15% fee**. Moving money isn't free.

## Loan sharks

- Borrow when you're desperate — but interest compounds.
- The shark is patient... until he isn't.
- Miss payments and consequences escalate. This is the game's pressure valve for broke players — high risk, high relief, high cost.
- **This is satire of predatory lending, not a metaphor for real-money microtransactions.** The game charges in-game currency. See [PRICING](../PRICING.md) for the real-world stance (no microtransactions, ever).

## Heat & arrest (economic impact)

See [Heat and Arrest](Heat-and-Arrest.md). When arrested: cash halved, car towed, court fines **double every offense**. Banking your money ahead of time is how you survive a bust.

## Phone apps

Your in-game phone runs:
- Bounties (income opportunities)
- Banking (with the 15% wire fee)
- Black market terminal
- Loan shark interface

Adult life on a screen — including the debt spiral.

## Design intent

The economy is hostile on purpose. It's not punishing for punishment's sake — it creates meaning around every fight, every purchase, every risk. When healing is free, fights are disposable. When healing costs, fights are *decisions*.

**No free healing.** It's a pillar. See [Governance](../GOVERNANCE.md) non-negotiables.
