# Lore

Worldbuilding for FEDMON: NO MERCY. This page expands over time.

> **Rule (non-negotiable):** No racially coded language in lore, names, or locations. See [ADR-005](../ADR.md). The setting is harsh; it is not a vehicle for stereotypes.

## The setting

A city where creature-taming is legal, regulated, and economically entangled. The "FED" in FEDMON refers to the jurisdictional/legal system that governs trainers, creatures, and property — the same system that arrests you when your heat gets too high.

## Tone

Gritty, fiscal, adult. The world doesn't romanticize poverty or crime — it presents them as systems that press on you. The loan shark isn't a cartoon villain; he's a pressure valve with teeth. The police aren't heroes or villains; they're a mechanical consequence of your choices.

## Themes (in-fiction)

- **Consequences over second chances.** Permanent skills, doubling fines, no free healing.
- **Money as survival.** Every system routes back to cash.
- **Ownership as liability.** Property is power and risk.
- **The body as a resource.** The trainer fights, so the trainer bleeds.

## What we avoid

- Racial or ethnic coding of crime, poverty, or violence.
- Romanticizing predatory lending or police violence as "cool."
- Treating arrest as a joke without consequences.
- Stereotypes of any real-world group.

The game is brutal. It is not bigoted. Those are different things.

## Placeholder names

Creature names (EMBERHOUND, VOLTHEDGE, SLICKLIZ, Blazehound, FED-Inferno, etc.) are working names and may evolve. If you propose lore/names in a discussion, run them past the no-coded-language rule before posting.
