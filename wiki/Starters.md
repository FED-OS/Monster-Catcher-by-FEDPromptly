# Starters

Three starting creatures. One busted crate. Pick your problem.

## EMBERHOUND — 🔥 Fire / 🐺 Brute

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 60 |
| ATK | 90 |
| DEF | 70 |
| SPD | 45 |
| SYNC | 80 |

**Evolution chain:** Emberhound → Blazehound → FED-Inferno

**Profile:** High-attack bruiser. Glass-cannon speed — hits hard, takes hits okay, but won't outpace a faster foe. Great for players who want to end fights fast and brutal.

## VOLTHEDGE — ⚡ Volt / 🦔 Guard

| SYNC | 70 |

**Evolution chain:** Volthedge → Voltcrag → FED-Bulwark

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| HP | 55 |
| ATK | 65 |
| DEF | 50 |
| SPD | 95 |

**Evolution chain:** Slickliz → Venomskink → FED-Miasma

**Profile:** Fast disruptor. Lowest HP but highest speed. Applies pressure, poisons, and dictates pacing. For players who want to control the fight, not tank it.

## The 4th-form mechanic

Each starter gets a **1-turn 4th form**: double the highest stat, halve the lowest. It's a desperation play — a single turn of extreme output at a steep cost. Use it to close a fight you'd otherwise lose, or to survive one you shouldn't.

See [Evolution](Evolution.md) for details.

## Choosing

There's no wrong pick — but there is a pick that fits your style:
- Aggressive → **EMBERHOUND**
- Defensive → **VOLTHEDGE**
- Tactical/speed → **SLICKLIZ**

Your trainer skills (see [Combat System](Combat-System.md)) layer on top, so the starter isn't your whole identity.
