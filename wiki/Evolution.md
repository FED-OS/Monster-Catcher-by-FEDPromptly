# Evolution

How creatures grow in FEDMON: NO MERCY.

## 3-stage chains

Every starter has a three-stage evolution:

| Starter | Stage 2 | Stage 3 (FED form) |
|---------|---------|--------------------|
| Emberhound | Blazehound | FED-Inferno |
| Volthedge | Voltcrag | FED-Bulwark |
| Slickliz | Venomskink | FED-Miasma |

The "FED-" prefix on the final form ties to the game's legal/jurisdictional themes (the "fed" identity — see the naming decision in [ADR-005](../ADR.md)).

## Leveling pace

Higher tiers level **slower**. The jump from stage 2 to stage 3 is a grind — intentional. Evolution is an achievement, not a checkbox.

## Starter 4th form (1-turn)

Each starter gets a **1-turn 4th form**:
- **Double** the highest stat.
- **Halve** the lowest stat.

It lasts one turn. It's a desperation mechanic — a moment of extreme output with a sharp trade-off. Use it to:
- Close a fight you'd otherwise lose.
- Survive a hit that would KO you.
- Set up a decisive ULT.

The cost (halving your lowest stat for that turn) means it's not a free win — it's a calculated all-in.

## Non-starter creatures

Beyond the starters, the world has more creatures to catch, trade, and (painfully) lose. The full roster expands over development — see [Roadmap](../ROADMAP.md).

## Design intent

- Evolution is **earned**, not handed out. Slow pacing makes it meaningful.
- The 4th form rewards knowing your creature's stat profile — you have to know which stat to double and which to sacrifice.
- No evolution items sold for real money (see [PRICING](../PRICING.md)).
