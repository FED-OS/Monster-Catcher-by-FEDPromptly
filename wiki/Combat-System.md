# Combat System

FEDMON: NO MERCY uses **dual combat**: your trainer and your creature fight together in the same encounter.

## Core idea

Most creature-collectors put the trainer on the sidelines. FEDMON doesn't. You throw hands alongside your monster. This means:
- You have your own HP and can be targeted.
- Your trainer skills are powerful but costly and permanent.
- The enemy can engage you OR your creature.

## Trainer skills

- Fists / melee — direct damage, no resource but your own HP risk.
- Skills cost **2×** what they'd cost a creature and are **permanent** — no relearn, no take-backs. Choose carefully.
- You can counter theft attempts mid-battle with a fistfight (if a thief tries to snatch your creature, you can throw down).

## Creature combat

- Standard move pools per type (fire, volt, toxin, etc.).
- SYNC energy builds through the fight and gates your **ULT** — a big signature move. (The promo page's combat HUD demos this: fill the SYNC bar to unleash ULT.)
- Damage is visible: floating numbers, sparks, screen shake.

## Moves (from the promo HUD)

| Move | Role |
|------|------|
| Jab | Quick light damage |
| Dodge | Avoid incoming, small SYNC gain |
| Tackle | Solid damage, moderate cost |
| Elemental Strike | Type-based burst |
| Synergy ULT | Big finisher — requires full SYNC |

## Why this design

- It makes the trainer a character, not a coach.
- It creates resource tension (your body is a resource).
- Permanent skills mean build identity matters — no free respecs.
